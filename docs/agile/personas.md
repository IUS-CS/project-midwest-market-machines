# Personas

## Persona A: James Holland

James Holland, age 36, is an early retiree turned consultant with a machine learning and robotics education from MIT. After escaping bosses, office politics, HR training, and schedule oversight, James moved to Montauk to spend his time outdoors and at the beach with his wife and two children, ages 7 and 10.

James is familiar with investing and is a competent trader. Through consulting work and active portfolio management, he is able to maintain his early retirement lifestyle.

James’ experience as a machine learning consultant makes managing digital technology second nature to him. However, he dislikes having to give up his free time to monitor stocks and manually execute trades. While he has more than enough experience to understand complex digital systems, he does not want the stress or cognitive overhead of traditional day-trading software weighing on his mind.

---

## Persona B: Rick Vasquez

Rick Vasquez, age 26, is a physical therapist assistant with a 9-to-5 job in Elon, North Carolina. His work most often involves caring for patients with sports injuries or age-related physical issues.

Rick was born in Charlotte but moved to Elon, where he is studying to earn a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree. His mother teaches English at the school, and his father is a dentist.

Rick does not have a formal financial education and lacks both the time and money to learn everything there is to know about the stock market. With a busy work schedule, he also does not have time to consistently read or watch financial news that may affect market behavior.

Rick hopes to use the Midwest Market Machine application to build a faster-growth portfolio without needing to establish long-term positions or hire financial experts.
