# User Stories

As a -role-, I want to -do something- so that -reason-.

---

## Retail Investor

### View current asset price

As a retail investor, I want to view the current price of an asset so that I can see its current valuation.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- The system displays the price with a maximum delay of 1 second from the market source.
- Prices are shown in the user’s preferred currency.

---

### View historical asset price

As a retail investor, I want to view the historical price of an asset so that I can track its performance over time.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- The interface provides selectable timeframes: 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, and all time.
- I can hover over points on the chart to see the exact price at a specific date and time.
- The graph automatically adjusts its Y-axis to fit the selected timeframe.

---

### Understand price movement over time

As a retail investor, I want to see how the price of an asset has moved over time so that I can predict trends and plan strategies.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Price changes are represented by color, green for gains and red for losses.
- The UI shows both the numerical price change and the percentage change.

---

### View real-time price changes

As a retail investor, I want to see how the price of an asset is currently changing so that I can decide whether to buy or sell.

---

### Search for assets

As a retail investor, I want to search for assets by name or ticker so that I can navigate the platform efficiently.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- The search bar provides auto-complete suggestions after two characters are entered.
- Search results include the full asset name and its ticker symbol.
- If no asset matches the query, the system displays a “No results found” message.

---

### Track favorite assets

As a retail investor, I want to track my favorite assets so that I can monitor them and build investment strategies.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- I can mark an asset as a favorite from the search results or asset detail page.
- A dedicated watchlist dashboard displays all favorited assets.
- I can remove assets from my watchlist at any time.

---

## High Net-Worth Investor

### Provide industry knowledge to AI

As a high net-worth industry expert investor, I want to inform an AI about a specific news source or asset so that I can leverage my industry knowledge for competitive strategies.

---

### Automate buying decisions

As a high net-worth industry expert investor, I want to automate the buying process so that I can capture short-term trading opportunities without constant manual effort.
